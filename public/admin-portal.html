Customer Upload

Upload your core banking CSV export to import customer accounts and balances. This creates new customer accounts if they don't exist.

📋 CSV Format Required: Account Number, Customer Type, Title, First Name, Middle Name, Surname, Gender, ID Type, ID Number, DOB, Home Address, Postal Address, Country, Email, Mobile Phone Number, Account Type, Account By Ownership, Product Name, Status Of Account, Account Branch, Currency Of Account, Account Balance

💡 Instructions

  1. Export customer data from your core banking system as CSV
  2. Ensure the CSV file includes all required columns (see format above)
  3. Click "Select CSV File" and choose your exported file
  4. Click "Upload Customer Data" to start the import
  5. Wait for the upload to complete - you'll see progress and results
  6. New customers will be created automatically
  7. Existing customer balances will be updated
⚠️ Important: Large files may take several minutes to process. Do not close this page during upload.
